Turbo Flying Car

Dodge desi traffic. Fly over it. Help everyone on the way.

About the game

Drive through busy Indian streets, change lanes between autos, buses and cows, and tap FLY to soar over the jam. Along the way, pick up people and animals who need help, drop them safely, follow the rules and beat the clock. Travel stage by stage across India, upgrade your car, build your town and earn medals.

  • Stages and levels across India, from city streets to coasts, deserts and mountains
  • Fly over traffic, with a flight battery that recharges
  • Rescue missions, traffic rules and a licence-points system
  • Car upgrades, cosmetics, medals and a town to build
  • Works offline; no ads, no accounts, no tracking
