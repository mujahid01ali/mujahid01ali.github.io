Turbo Flying Car

Privacy Policy · Effective 30 October 2026

The short version

  • Turbo Flying Car does not collect, store or share your personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your progress is saved only on your own device and never sent to us.

Who we are

Turbo Flying Car is a driving game for iPhone and iPad, also playable in a web browser. It is made by Mujahid Ali (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

Information saved on your device

To remember your game, Turbo Flying Car saves the following only on your device (in the app’s own storage on iPhone and iPad, or in your browser’s local storage on the web):

  • the player name you type in, your chosen avatar, home state and trip start place;
  • game progress: coins, diamonds, stars, levels, licence points, town buildings, cars, cosmetics, medals and records;
  • settings such as sound, speed, controls and reduced motion.

This information is never uploaded or sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset all progress in the game, by deleting the app, or by clearing the website’s data in your browser.

Tip: you don’t need to use your real name; any nickname works.

Photos

On iPhone and iPad, if you tap Save result card, the app asks for permission to add one picture to your photo library. This permission only lets the app save images; it cannot see, read or upload your photos. You can change this at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Sharing

When you tap Share run, your device’s own share sheet opens with your result card and a short message. You choose where it goes (for example a messaging app). We do not receive anything you share. Whatever you share is then handled by the app or service you choose.

Internet connections

iPhone and iPad app: the game works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.

Web version: like any website, the server hosting the page receives standard request information (such as your IP address and browser type) when the page loads. The web version also loads its font from Google Fonts and its 3D graphics library from the jsDelivr network, which receive the same standard request information under their own privacy policies. We do not add cookies, analytics or trackers.

Children

Turbo Flying Car is suitable for players of all ages. Because the game collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children. A player name typed into the game stays on the device and is never sent anywhere.

Third-party services

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, social media or tracking code. If you download it from the App Store, Apple’s own privacy policy covers your App Store account and downloads.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Because we don’t collect contact details, we can’t notify you directly, so please check this page from time to time.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game? Open an issue at github.com/mujahid01ali/mujahid01ali.github.io/issues.
